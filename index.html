HOW TO PLAY

Choose your judgement for each claim.

Supported means the supplied evidence is enough. Unsupported or incorrect means the claim conflicts with evidence or makes an unjustified assertion. Needs qualification means it could be reasonable only with a condition, missing fact or narrower wording. The point is to explain your decision, not guess a label.
